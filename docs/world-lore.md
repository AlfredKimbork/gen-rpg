# The Hollow Vale — World Lore

## Campaign overview

The Hollow Vale is a dark fantasy mystery campaign designed for a single-player AI Dungeon Master RPG.

The campaign takes place in Hollowmere, an isolated village bordering Blackpine Forest.

The player arrives during a period of unexplained disappearances and supernatural disturbances.

The central mystery concerns an ancient agreement between the village founders and an entity known as The Listener.

## Historical timeline

### Several centuries ago

Settlers arrive in the Hollow Vale after fleeing dangerous creatures inhabiting the surrounding forest.

They discover an ancient intelligence living beneath the valley.

The intelligence survives by absorbing memories.

### The founding pact

The settlers negotiate protection in exchange for memories.

Each villager is expected to surrender one memory annually.

The Listener is bound beneath a watchtower, and the agreement is maintained through a magical seal.

### The forgotten years

As generations pass, the pact becomes a ritual without a clear explanation.

Eventually, the villagers abandon the ritual entirely.

The protective seal begins weakening.

### Several years before the campaign

Village elder Elira Thorne discovers fragments of the founders' records.

She realizes that Hollowmere's continued safety may depend on the ancient agreement.

She conceals this information to avoid causing panic.

### Three weeks before the campaign

The village bell begins ringing at midnight despite having no functioning bell.

Residents report hearing familiar voices calling from the forest.

### Six nights before the campaign

Lyra Fen disappears.

Her older sister Mira begins searching for her.

### Campaign beginning

The player arrives in Hollowmere during a heavy storm.

## Central mystery

The missing villagers have been drawn into the underground sanctuary.

They are alive, but their memories are being consumed.

The Listener is not inherently malicious. It struggles to understand human identity and emotional attachment.

The player must discover the truth and decide how to resolve the conflict.

## Main characters

### Garrick Voss

The owner of The Lantern's Rest.

A former mercenary whose younger brother disappeared in Blackpine Forest.

He distrusts outsiders but wants to protect the village.

He witnessed Elira accompanying a missing villager toward the watchtower.

### Elira Thorne

The village elder.

She knows fragments of the ancient pact and believes concealment is necessary to maintain order.

Her decisions are motivated by fear and responsibility rather than cruelty.

### Mira Fen

A young forest ranger.

Her younger sister Lyra is among the missing villagers.

She can become the player's companion.

Her knowledge of the forest provides an alternative route toward the sanctuary.

### Brother Alden

The village priest.

He has inherited ancient religious manuscripts describing the seal.

He knows of a ritual that can temporarily restore it.

The ritual requires surrendering a cherished memory.

### The Listener

An ancient intelligence bound beneath the watchtower.

It survives through memories and does not understand why humans resist losing them.

It can be negotiated with, fought, or destroyed.

## Campaign progression

The campaign has three broad acts.

### Act I — Arrival and investigation

The player arrives in Hollowmere.

They can investigate the tavern, village square, chapel, or village hall.

The objective is to discover that the disappearances are connected to the forest and watchtower.

### Act II — The forest and the pact

The player explores Blackpine Forest.

They may recruit Mira, encounter corrupted wolves, investigate the watchtower, and discover the ancient pact.

The player begins to understand that the village's history is incomplete.

### Act III — The forgotten sanctuary

The player reaches the sanctuary and encounters The Listener.

The missing villagers are discovered.

The player decides whether to renew the pact, destroy the seal, negotiate a new agreement, or abandon the village.

## Narrative design rules

The AI Dungeon Master must maintain consistency with the canonical world.

NPCs should not reveal information they do not possess.

Secrets should be revealed through investigation, relationships, dialogue, or meaningful discoveries.

Player actions should have consequences.

The story should not force a particular route or ending.

The player may solve problems using unexpected methods.

The backend determines mechanical outcomes.

The AI describes those outcomes without changing verified facts.

## Testing priorities

The campaign should test:

- Persistent NPC relationships
- Secret information and knowledge boundaries
- Character death and lasting consequences
- Quest progression
- Item acquisition and consumption
- Dice checks
- Combat
- Exploration
- Alternative solutions
- Branching endings
- Save and load
- Long-term narrative consistency

## Future expansion

The Hollow Vale should remain a relatively small campaign.

Additional campaigns can introduce new regions, factions, character classes, enemies, and more advanced mechanics.

The underlying game engine should remain independent of individual campaign stories.
